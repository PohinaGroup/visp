Portrait Direct output turns one landscape contribution into two independent
platform outputs. The landscape destination keeps the original frame. The
portrait destination uses a manual 9:16 crop and its own relay forwarder.

Portrait output is optional. An operator turns it on per account; until then the
portrait controls do not appear. See [Direct output](/docs/direct-output) for
the landscape setup this builds on.

## Add and frame portrait

1. Authorize two Direct destinations and enable the landscape destination.
2. On the dashboard **Destinations** tab, choose **Add portrait output** for
   another authorized provider. In the phone app's stream settings the button
   is **Add portrait**.
3. Move and resize the crop over the part of the landscape preview viewers
   should see. The portrait preview shows the resulting 9:16 frame.
4. Choose **Save framing**, then go live.

The crop is saved to that portrait destination. **Edit framing** changes the
next encode; a failed save keeps the editor open so you can retry. The controls
also work from a keyboard or accessibility input.

## Slots and failures

Landscape and portrait consume one forwarder slot each. Configuration is
allowed when the relay is full, with a warning. At Go Live, a full relay blocks
portrait but allows landscape to proceed. A failure or retry on one destination
does not stop the other.

Removing portrait stops using its slot. Use a different provider for portrait:
publishing two frames to the same account stream key would make the platform
disconnect one of them.
