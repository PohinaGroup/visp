# VISP Pro Hardware Reviewer Affiliate Program

## Goal

Turn trusted IRL-streaming setup reviews into retained VISP Pro subscribers.
Start with a 90-day, invite-only pilot for five YouTube creators who publish
hands-on backpack, bonding, mobile encoder, or remote-production content.

## Pilot offer

- **Commission:** 25% of net VISP Pro subscription revenue for each referred
  customer's first 12 paid months.
- **Attribution:** 30 days from the creator's last affiliate-link click. A
  creator code is the fallback when cookies are unavailable.
- **Payout:** Monthly, after a 30-day refund and chargeback hold, once the
  creator has at least EUR 50 payable. Refunds and chargebacks are deducted.
- **Access:** Complimentary VISP Pro while the creator remains in the program.
- **Editorial terms:** Honest opinions, no required script, and no approval of
  conclusions. VISP may only check unpublished content for factual errors.
- **Disclosure:** Every placement must clearly identify the affiliate
  relationship and follow YouTube's paid-promotion rules and applicable law.

"Net revenue" means subscription revenue actually collected, excluding VAT or
sales tax, refunds, chargebacks, and payment-processing fees. Self-referrals,
brand bidding, spam, misleading claims, and cookie stuffing are not eligible.

## Who to recruit

Prioritize creators who meet all of these criteria:

1. Have published an IRL backpack, mobile bonding, SRT, BELABOX, Moblin, or
   remote OBS setup video in the last 12 months.
2. Demonstrate hardware on a real stream instead of only reading specifications.
3. Receive substantive setup and buying questions in their comments.
4. Publish in English, Finnish, or another market VISP can currently support.
5. Are willing to test VISP before recommending it.

Audience fit and viewer trust matter more than subscriber count. Exclude coupon
channels, generic software roundups, and creators asking for guaranteed praise.

Track candidates in a simple sheet with: channel, contact, relevant video,
average views on the last five relevant videos, audience geography, outreach
date, status, affiliate code, and notes.

## Creator package

Each accepted creator receives:

- a Pro test account and a 30-minute technical onboarding;
- one unique link and memorable creator code;
- a one-page facts sheet covering supported inputs, outputs, latency tradeoffs,
  pricing, and known limitations;
- test scenarios: phone-only stream, backpack/SRT stream, connection drop, and
  OBS handoff;
- direct access to one VISP contact for factual and setup questions;
- monthly clicks, trials, paid conversions, retained subscribers, and commission.

Do not require a dedicated video. Accept a build video, setup update, comparison,
description link, or pinned comment when VISP is genuinely relevant.

## Funnel and attribution

Use the billing provider's native affiliate or coupon support when VISP Pro is
implemented. Do not build custom payout infrastructure for the pilot.

The minimum funnel is:

`creator link/code -> VISP Pro landing page -> account -> paid subscription`

Persist the affiliate identifier through signup and checkout. Credit the last
eligible affiliate touch within 30 days. Keep immutable records of the credited
creator, customer, payment, refund, and commission so payout totals are
auditable. The privacy policy and consent flow must describe referral tracking
before launch.

## Outreach

Subject: Test VISP in your next IRL streaming build

> Hi [name] — your [specific video/build] is exactly the kind of real-world
> setup VISP is built for. VISP sends a phone or backpack feed through an
> SRT/RTMP relay to Twitch, Kick, YouTube, or OBS. We're preparing VISP Pro and
> inviting five hardware-focused creators to test it without a required script
> or positive-review clause. Partners receive free Pro access and 25% of net
> subscription revenue from referred customers for their first 12 paid months.
> Would you be open to a short setup session and an honest test?

Send no more than two follow-ups. Personalize the first sentence with a real
detail from the creator's setup; bulk outreach is a poor fit for this niche.

## Pilot operation

1. Finalize Pro pricing, included usage, supported countries, and billing
   provider.
2. Approve a short affiliate agreement covering commission, attribution,
   disclosure, prohibited promotion, termination, tax details, and data use.
3. Recruit and onboard five creators before public Pro launch.
4. Give each creator at least two weeks to test on real hardware.
5. Review results 30, 60, and 90 days after the first content goes live.
6. Continue creators who bring retained customers; stop inactive or misleading
   placements without expanding the program.

## Scorecard

Review per creator and in total:

- affiliate-link clicks;
- account and checkout conversion;
- paid subscribers and net monthly recurring revenue;
- 30- and 90-day subscriber retention;
- refunds and chargebacks;
- commission paid;
- support time and any fixed creator cost;
- customer acquisition cost and first-year contribution after commission.

Expand beyond five creators only when referred customers retain at least as
well as other Pro customers and the pilot recovers all commission, creator, and
support costs within 12 months. Otherwise adjust the product, offer, or creator
profile before adding tooling or headcount.
